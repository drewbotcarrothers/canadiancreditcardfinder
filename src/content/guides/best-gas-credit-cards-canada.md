**If you drive a lot, a card that pays a higher rate at the pump can easily return more than its annual fee. In Canada, the strongest cash back rates on gas are 3% to 4%, but almost every one comes with an annual cap or a list of merchants that count.** This guide walks through the main options, how the caps work, and how to stack a card with a fuel-loyalty program.

## What counts as "gas"

Card issuers decide what counts as gas using the merchant category code (MCC) the station's payment terminal reports. That usually covers stand-alone gas stations and automated fuel pumps. It often doesn't cover fuel bought at a grocery store or warehouse club that codes the whole store as one merchant, the snacks and car wash bought inside if they're rung up separately, or a station attached to a big-box store.

A few cards now include **EV charging** in the gas category. CIBC's Dividend cards, for example, include electric vehicle charging (MCC 5552) alongside gas stations. If you drive electric, check that wording before you choose.

## Fuel-loyalty programs you can stack

A card's cash back and a gas station's loyalty program are usually separate, so you can often collect both:

- **Canadian Tire Gas+ and Petro-Canada.** The [Triangle World Elite Mastercard](/card/triangle-world-elite-mastercard/) ($0) lists 7¢/L in CT Money on premium fuel and 5¢/L on other fuel at Gas+, with an $80,000 personal or $150,000 household income requirement. The base [Triangle Mastercard](/card/triangle-mastercard/) also earns fuel cents per litre at Gas+ and Petro-Canada.
- **Petro-Canada with RBC.** RBC cards such as the [RBC Visa Platinum](/card/rbc-visa-platinum/) list savings of 3¢/L at Petro-Canada when you link an eligible RBC card, plus 20% more Petro-Points.
- **Journie Rewards with CIBC.** CIBC cards list up to 10 cents off per litre at participating Pioneer, Fas Gas, Ultramar and Chevron stations when you link a Journie Rewards card.

## How to work out what a gas card is worth

1. Look at a few months of statements and add up what you spend at gas stations (or EV chargers).
2. Multiply by the card's gas rate, but stop at the cap. For example, $3,000 a year on the TD Cash Back Visa Infinite at 3% is $90. Over $15,000, every extra dollar earns 1%.
3. Add what the card earns on your other categories, such as groceries, since most gas cards also pay well there.
4. Subtract the annual fee. A $120 card has to beat a no-fee card by more than $120 a year to be worth it.

If you spend modestly on gas, a no-fee card like the SimplyCash or Simplii may come out ahead once the fee is counted. If you're on the road constantly, watch the caps: a 4% card with a combined cap can run out faster than you expect when groceries share the same limit.

## FAQ

**Do warehouse club gas stations count as gas?**
It depends on how the station codes. The CIBC Costco Mastercard pays a specific 3% at Costco gas in Canada; on other cards, check whether the issuer lists the merchant as a gas station.

**Does EV charging earn the gas rate?**
On some cards. CIBC Dividend cards, the TD Cash Back Visa Infinite and the CIBC Costco Mastercard all list EV charging alongside gas.

**Can I earn cash back and Petro-Points on the same fill-up?**
Usually yes. You scan or link the loyalty card and pay with your credit card, and each program awards its own rewards.
