**Aeroplan and Scene+ are the two points programs Canadians ask about most. The short version: Aeroplan is an airline program whose points can be worth more when you book the right flights, while Scene+ is a fixed-value program where 100 points are generally worth $1 on travel and groceries.** Neither is better in every case. The right one depends on whether you want to work for extra value or just want points that are simple to use.

This guide compares the two programs side by side and shows which credit cards earn each one. For a deeper look at each program on its own, see [Aeroplan for beginners](/guides/aeroplan-for-beginners-canada/) and [Scene+ explained](/guides/scene-plus-explained/).

## The core difference: variable value vs fixed value

**Aeroplan** is Air Canada's loyalty program. Reward flights on Air Canada use dynamic pricing, so the number of points for a seat moves with demand, and partner airlines are priced from a distance-based chart. That means the value of a point changes from booking to booking. You can measure it with a simple formula:

**(Cash fare − taxes and fees you still pay) ÷ points needed × 100 = cents per point**

TD's Aeroplan card page says the majority of its cardholders received $0.02 per point or more on flight redemptions. That's a useful benchmark, but it's not guaranteed: a cheap economy flight booked at a high points price can be worth much less.

**Scene+** is co-owned by Scotiabank, Cineplex and Empire (the parent company of Sobeys, Safeway, IGA, FreshCo and Foodland). In its common redemptions, such as Scene+ Travel, Apply Points to Travel on a Scotiabank Scene+ card, and groceries at Sobeys-family stores, 100 points equal $1. A point is worth about a cent, and that doesn't change with seat availability.

| | Aeroplan | Scene+ |
|---|---|---|
| Type of program | Airline (Air Canada + partners) | Retail, banking and travel coalition |
| Typical value | Varies by booking; TD cites $0.02+ for most of its cardholders' flight redemptions | 100 points = $1 on most travel and grocery redemptions |
| Best redemptions | Flights, especially long-haul and premium cabins | Any travel charged to the card, groceries at Sobeys-family stores, Cineplex |
| Airline transfer partners | It *is* the airline program | None |
| Main card issuers | TD, CIBC, American Express | Scotiabank |

## How each one fits real travel

Aeroplan works best when you can be flexible about dates and routes, and when the trips you want are expensive in cash. A family flying to Europe in summer, or anyone hoping to fly business class on points, usually gets more from Aeroplan.

Scene+ works best when you want to redeem against any travel purchase without hunting for award seats. Because Apply Points to Travel lets you book with an airline, a hotel's own site or a travel agent, charge it to a Scotiabank Scene+ card and then redeem within 12 months of the purchase, it suits people who book cheap flights, road trips, cottages or hotels outside an airline ecosystem.

## Which cards earn each program

**Aeroplan cards** are issued by TD, CIBC and American Express. Mid-tier options such as the [TD Aeroplan Visa Infinite](/card/td-aeroplan-visa-infinite-card/) and [CIBC Aeroplan Visa Infinite](/card/cibc-aeroplan-visa-infinite-card/) carry a $139 annual fee and earn 1.5 points per $1 on gas, EV charging, groceries and purchases made directly with Air Canada, and 1 point per $1 on everything else. Both list a $60,000 personal or $100,000 household income requirement. TD's version adds a free first checked bag on Air Canada flights for you and up to 8 companions on the same reservation. The no-fee [CIBC Aeroplan Visa](/card/cibc-aeroplan-visa-card/) earns 1 point per $1 in those categories with a $15,000 household income minimum. American Express Membership Rewards points (earned on cards like the [Cobalt](/card/american-express-cobalt-card/)) can also be transferred to Aeroplan at 1:1.

**Scene+ cards** are issued by Scotiabank. The [Scotiabank Gold American Express](/card/scotiabank-gold-american-express-card/) ($120) earns 6x at Sobeys-family grocers, 5x on other groceries, dining, food delivery and entertainment, and 3x on gas, daily transit and select streaming, on the first $50,000 a year. It also has no foreign transaction fees. The [Scotiabank Passport Visa Infinite](/card/scotiabank-passport-visa-infinite-card/) ($150) earns 3x at Sobeys-family grocers and 2x on dining, other groceries, entertainment and daily transit, with no foreign transaction fees and six lounge passes a year. The no-fee [Scotiabank American Express Card](/card/scotiabank-american-express-card/) earns 3x at Sobeys-family stores and 2x on other groceries, dining, entertainment, gas, transit and select streaming.

## Comparing earn rates fairly

Don't compare "5x" to "1.5x" without converting both into dollars. A rough way to do it:

- **Scene+:** points × 1 cent. On the Scotiabank Gold Amex, $100 at a non-Sobeys grocery store earns 500 points, which is about $5 back.
- **Aeroplan:** points × the value you realistically get. On a TD or CIBC Aeroplan Visa Infinite, $100 of groceries earns 150 points. At 2 cents a point that's $3; at 1.5 cents it's $2.25.

On grocery and dining spending, Scene+ cards can come out ahead even at a fixed 1 cent. Aeroplan cards make up the gap with travel perks such as checked bags, and with the chance to redeem at a higher value.

## Expiry rules

Under Aeroplan's inactivity policy, points expire after 18 months with no activity, but they don't expire while you're a primary holder of a TD, CIBC or American Express Aeroplan card. Aeroplan has paused expiry until November 30, 2026.

Scene+ points don't expire while you hold an eligible Scotiabank product. Without one, they expire after 24 months of inactivity.

## Which should you choose?

Pick **Aeroplan** if:

- You fly Air Canada or Star Alliance partners at least once or twice a year.
- You're willing to compare points prices with cash fares before you book.
- Checked bags, preferred pricing and lounge-type perks matter to you.

Pick **Scene+** if:

- You shop at Sobeys, Safeway, IGA, FreshCo or Foodland.
- You want a fixed value you can use on any travel without searching for award seats.
- You spend in foreign currency and want a card with no foreign transaction fees.

Plenty of households hold one card from each program: a Scene+ card for groceries and dining, and an Aeroplan card for Air Canada bookings and the bag benefit.

## FAQ

**Can I transfer Scene+ points to Aeroplan?**
No. Scene+ doesn't have airline transfer partners.

**Is an Aeroplan point worth more than a Scene+ point?**
Often, on flights. TD says most of its cardholders got $0.02 per point or more on flight redemptions, while Scene+ is generally 1 cent per point. On non-flight redemptions, Aeroplan values are usually lower.

**Which is easier for a beginner?**
Scene+. The value is fixed and you can redeem against any travel charged to your Scotiabank Scene+ card.
