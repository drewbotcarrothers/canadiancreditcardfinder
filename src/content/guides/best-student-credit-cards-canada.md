**The best student credit card in Canada is a $0 card you will be approved for, that works at the tills you actually use, and that you pay in full.** Rewards are a tie-breaker. [Scotiabank Scene+ Visa (for students)](/card/scotiabank-scene-plus-visa-card-for-students/) fits Empire grocers, Home Hardware, and Cineplex on a Visa. [Student BMO CashBack Mastercard](/card/student-bmo-cashback-mastercard/) fits supermarket cash back and Costco. [CIBC Dividend Visa (for students)](/card/cibc-dividend-visa-card-for-students/) is the Dividend cash-back path. [Scotiabank American Express (for students)](/card/scotiabank-american-express-card-for-students/) pays a stronger Scene+ grocery/dining mix if the merchant takes Amex.

You are not late if you open a card in second year. You are early if you open three in September. One card, on-time payments, low utilization, is how a student file gets built. The live list is on the [students hub](/best/students/).

This is not a way to finance tuition. Student purchase rates in our data are ordinary rewards rates, not “student discounts” on interest.

## Eligibility before rewards

Canadian student cards usually require enrolment in a certificate, diploma, or degree program at a recognized post-secondary school. Some rows add an age band (BMO’s student CashBack review currently describes 18–24). Some require Canadian citizenship or permanent residence (several Scotiabank student rows). Some accept income from a job, student loan, scholarship, or family allowance because issuers know you may not have a salary.

**Read the eligibility cell on the card page.** International students should not assume a “student” product will take a study permit. CIBC and BMO rows are often the first place to look if Scotia’s citizenship line blocks you. Approval is still not guaranteed.

Parents sometimes want an authorized user card instead. That can help a household shop. It does not always build the student’s own file the same way a primary card does. Confirm how the issuer reports authorized users.

## Scene+ on campus

Scotiabank’s student Scene+ Visa currently lists $0, 1x everywhere, and 2x at eligible Sobeys-family grocers plus 2x at eligible Home Hardware and Cineplex in the student review — a thicker campus list than the adult Scene+ Visa centres. Confirm it is still live on the student page.

The welcome path in that review is staged (a smaller Scene+ amount after a modest three-month spend, then more at a higher gate). The two-year Scotiabank personal-card exclusion applies if you care about those points. Current and former primary or secondary cardholders, product-changers, and employees get cut.

If dining is the campus month and tills take Amex, the student Scotia Amex currently lists a stronger grocery and dining/transit mix at $0. Keep a Visa anyway. Residence convenience stores and small pizza counters are where Amex dies.

Redeem Scene+ where you already spend (movies, groceries) rather than hoarding for a trip you will take in 2029.

## Cash back on campus

[Student BMO CashBack](/card/student-bmo-cashback-mastercard/) currently mirrors the adult BMO CashBack shape (3% / 1% / 0.5% in that review) at $0, with extra cardholders free in our data and a first-three-months cash-back boost in the welcome line. The 18–24 student bar is the constraint. When you age out, expect a move toward adult [BMO CashBack](/card/bmo-cashback-mastercard/) or a conversation about World Elite if income appears.

Mastercard matters: Costco Canada will take it; a student Visa will not.

[CIBC Dividend Visa for students](/card/cibc-dividend-visa-card-for-students/) is cash back on Visa. Compare the live student grocery rate with BMO’s 3% and with Tangerine if you do not need a student product at all. Some students have enough file and income for an adult $0 card and should skip the student label.

[Scotia Momentum No-Fee for students](/card/scotia-momentum-no-fee-visa-card-for-students/) is the cash-back Scotia alternative if you do not use Scene+.

## What a student card is for

A student card is a **thin-file product**. The useful outcomes are:

- On-time payments reported to Canadian credit bureaus
- A credit limit you do not max out (utilization matters)
- One network you can use for rent-adjacent bills that accept cards, groceries, and a transit load

The unhelpful outcomes are a balance at the purchase rate, a late fee, and three inquiries from applying everywhere during orientation week.

If a parent will pay the bill, put the card in the student’s name only if the student will still see the statement. Hidden utilization is how files get wrecked quietly.

## After graduation

Issuers convert student products to adult versions. That can mean:

- A new annual fee
- A different earn table
- A new income test for Infinite or World Elite
- A credit-limit review

Read the conversion letter. If the adult fee is real and you do not need the card, downgrade or cancel after you have another open account in good standing. Do not cancel your only card the month you apply for a car loan.

Adult $0 cards — Tangerine, Simplii, PC Mastercard — are the usual landing zone. Paid Infinites wait until grocery spend and income justify them. See [cash back](/guides/best-cash-back-credit-cards-canada/) and [no annual fee](/guides/best-no-annual-fee-credit-cards-canada/).

## Common mistakes

- Applying because a campus table offered a tote bag. The inquiry stays.
- Using the card for tuition on a payment plan you cannot clear by the due date.
- Holding only an Amex on a campus that still prefers Visa.
- Ignoring citizenship lines and collecting declines.
- Treating Scene+ welcome points as cash in a budget.
